Research Outcomes & Awards

Expected project outputs

  • A precise problem statement and mathematical formulation.
  • Documented implementation and reproducible settings.
  • Comparative results with suitable evaluation metrics.
  • Analysis of failure cases and limitations.
  • A realistic research continuation plan.

30-day follow-up

Within 15 days: Submit interim results for mentor feedback.

Within 30 days: Submit the refined project report and code.

Carefully verified negative findings are valid outcomes.

Best Project Presentation Award

Presented for an outstanding final project presentation.

Best Research Project Awards

Presented to three outstanding participants.

Detailed assessment criteria will be announced before the school.

Selected reports may be developed into book chapters after further revision. Publication depends on editorial review and publisher acceptance.
